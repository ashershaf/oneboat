Carrying This Together

Israel is ours. All of ours. Not a cause we support from the outside, but a home we are responsible for, together.

Tell us how you'd like to help See the ways to take part

For generations, the division was clear: the people in Israel were the people on the ground, and people and institutions around the world supported their initiatives financially. That giving matters; it always has, and it always will. What we're proposing adds to it: an equally real, equally ongoing form of partnership. Ownership is something you express by staying actively present in Israel's life.

This is the new reality

What used to be the exception is becoming the rule

  • Reserve duty, once measured in weeks, is now a fixture of daily life for hundreds of thousands of families.
  • Wounded soldiers face years of rehabilitation, not weeks.
  • Those living with PTSD are not recovering for a season; they carry it for life.
  • Threats sit on multiple fronts at once.

What was once treated as an emergency to get through is, for the foreseeable future, simply how things are.

It's still yours

Ownership doesn't stop at the border

Most people around the world are not moving to Israel. But anyone can come to Israel at any time and call it home: celebrate a bar or bat mitzvah there, spend a year learning, visit whenever they choose, and know that if they ever needed to, they could go.

If it's yours, its future is something you carry too, as a partner.

The Goal

What everyone can do

The goal is for everyone to take part: substantially, meaningfully, and on an ongoing basis. Not once, and not only when the news demands it. There are many real ways to do that.

Give consistently

Financial support, given on an ongoing basis, is one of the real, needed ways to take part.

Teach

English, a skill, a subject, over Zoom, every week, to kids.

Mental health

Israel is in desperate need of these professionals, on Zoom too.

Consult

Bring your professional expertise to Israeli students, institutions, and businesses that need exactly what you know.

Show up

A few weeks a year in agriculture, or on a mission built around your specific skill: medicine, PT, whatever you bring.

Organize

Rally your community to stand behind a specific group of reservist families, ongoing, not just once.

If you're in Israel
for the year

Spending a gap year or a semester in Israel? Take on one family: a reservist household, a bereaved family, a wounded soldier, and come to them every week. One relationship, kept up all year, changes what that year means for both of you.

What we're building

A platform for a sustained connection

One place that shows, in real time, what Israel actually needs: which community, which family, which institution, and connects each need to the person, group, or family abroad whose time, expertise, or ongoing partnership fits it. Not a single act, but a sustained connection, matched to what you have to offer and where it's needed most.
